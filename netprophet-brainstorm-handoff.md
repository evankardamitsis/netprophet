# NetProphet — brainstorm handoff (2026-10-02)

Paste this as the first message in a Chat conversation.

## Context
NetProphet (netprophet.gr) was a Greek amateur-tennis coins-and-predictions app. The betting
product and the "daily run" card game built on top of it are now considered obsolete. We are
brainstorming a NEW product. Vision: elevate the amateur sports scene and make it better.
Tennis in Athens is the starting point because we have the network (~1,500 player roster,
mailing groups, real match history), but we are not limited to tennis.

## Fixed constraints
- The business cannot rest on tournament organisers, club owners, or any micro-advertising
  (local sponsors included). Players pay.
- Easy, swift, "addictive". No betting connotations, no gambling vocabulary (Greek or English).
- Never sell advantage; nothing purchasable moves anyone up a ranking.
- Both languages, Greek first and genuinely good.

## Where the thinking landed so far
Concept: Fantasy minus the training, polls as the engine, recognition as the product.
- Weekly "η τριάδα σου": pick three players from the scene who you think win this week.
  No budget, no transfers, one decision, 20 seconds.
- Daily poll (10 seconds): "Καλύτερο backhand στα νότια προάστια;" Opinion, never wrong,
  needs no results feed.
- Sunday "ποιος κέρδισε;" quick check when results land; trios settle.
- Month end: crowd awards. "Backhand της εβδομάδας" lands on the player's profile. The
  recognition is the product and the screenshot of it is the distribution.
- Pays: picker buys a season pass (pick history, standings in their παρέα). The player being
  talked about pays for the profile layer (awards shelf, trend). Neither touches the ranking.
- Portable by design to padel, five-a-side, basketball: pick three, vote daily, crown the
  crowd's favourites. Tennis is only the first roster.
- Proposed first test before building: run the daily poll in existing WhatsApp/mailing groups
  for two weeks with no app. If people vote and argue, the engine works.

## What I want from this conversation
Interactive examples and mockups: screens, the weekly loop, copy in Greek, and alternatives
to the angle above. Push back on it; I am not sure of the angle or the end product yet.
