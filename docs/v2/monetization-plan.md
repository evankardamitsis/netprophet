# NetProphet: monetization plan v3

## Constraints
- Players pay.
- Nothing rests on clubs, courts or tournament organisers.
- No betting.
- Never sell advantage: money never buys points or ranking.
- Multi-sport: tennis first, then padel, basketball, football.
- Ads are allowed as one line.

## Goal
- **North Star:** weekly active users.
- **Viable:** about 75k€/yr.
- **12 months:** 10k weekly actives in tennis and padel, Athens.
- **24 months:** 25k weekly actives (about 60k MAU) across 3+ sports.

## Revenue lines

### 1. Pro subscription (core; replaces the Season Pass)
Pro is "your season assistant". It sells real value to the player, not just more numbers.
1. **Your tournaments in one place:** every tournament for your level and area, entry deadlines, and alerts when the draw and your match time are out. **This gets built first.** It uses the match sources we already ingest.
2. **Scouting before your own match:** form, history, surfaces, record against people you know. Only for your own match, so votes stay fair.
3. **Analysis of your game:** where you win and where you lose, e.g. third sets, surfaces, left-handers.
4. **Your real level numbers:** how they move and the distance to the next tier.
5. **Recognition:** a card for every win, a season recap, a profile worth showing.
- **Plus:** 12 daily quiz cards instead of 6.
- **Later, not now:** auto-highlights from phone video (SwingVision-style, $96 to $300/yr). It is a big build, so it comes later or through a partner.
- **Price:** 8,99€ a month (decided 8/10/2026). This sits with the "real value" apps: UTR about $10, Playtomic Premium $9.49, Strava about $6.70. Information-only apps charge 1 to 3€.

### 2. Small purchases
- Freezes for the σερί and gifts to a friend (a coffee 0,99€, a week of Pro).
- All cosmetics for the avatar and profile, at 0,99 to 1,99€ each:
  - backgrounds (7)
  - frames (10)
  - outfits (6)
  - win effects (3)
  - future sets per sport and per season
- Some cosmetics are earned for free through Αποστολές and badges. The rest are bought. None of them affect points.
- Euro prices only: no coins, no random boxes.
- About 0,40€ per MAU per year.

### 3. Ads / native placements
- Few, inside the feed.
- About 0,40€ per MAU per year at scale (FuPa 0,40, FotMob 0,52).

### 4. Later stage: the people around the player
- Friends, family and teammates who follow a player can pay too (the GameChanger lesson: families pay about $180/yr).
- They arrive through players sharing matches and win cards, not through contact lists. Revenue for after launch.

### 5. Later stage: tournament organisers
- Organisers pay a fee to have their tournament hosted on NetProphet: draws, schedule, results, and reaching the players.
- It's an extra line once we have the players. The plan does not depend on it.

## Model (€/yr, low / base / high)

|---|---|---|---|

Base assumes 2.5% pay, which is unproven.

## Tests before building
1. **Questionnaire:**
   - Ask 10 to 15 roster players: "Which of the 5 Pro points would you pay for, and how much?"
   - Cost: zero.
2. **Fake door in the prototype:**
   - Share the prototype (or a 30-second video) with 50 to 100 roster players.
   - At the end, a «Γίνε μέλος» button shows a price (3,99 or 7,99).
   - Pressing it says "not yet, you're on the list".
   - No money is taken. We measure who presses, at which price.
3. **Paywall inside the free app** after launch, for real numbers.

## Dropped
- Season Pass, replaced by Pro.
- Court booking through clubs.
- Ladder as the main line.
- Private leagues: too much admin, and they compete with organisers.
- Pre-sale with no product.

## Open
- Number of 5x5 and amateur basketball leagues in Athens.
- Greek CPM.
- Consent and data rules for junior players.
