## Things to check before pasting

- **«ενημερωμένα μόνα τους μέσα στη μέρα»**: the schedules are checked three times a day (M4); results arrive from
  the sheets and from staff. If the founder wants a number, «τρεις φορές τη μέρα» is what the 16 Oct draft said.
- **«Κράτα πατημένο ένα όνομα»**: the hold-a-name player run is live on Πρόγραμμα, Ταμπλό and Σήμερα (21 Oct).
- **Pro list**: matches `pro.outcomes` and `pro.also` in `packages/copy/src/el.ts`. «Τα τουρνουά σου» alerts are
  for the tournaments of your claimed player (STATUS «Pro: real»), which is what «τα ματς σου» says.
- **Founding plan**: the description says «μηνιαία ή ετήσια», which covers `pro_founding` and `pro_yearly`. If the
  founding offer stays a separate product, do not promise in the listing that it renews at a different price.
- **Name**: «NetProphet: Τένις» ties the name to tennis; fine for 1.0, rename when padel or another sport ships.

ASC computes and change the policy to "13 and over" only if that tier comes out, or raise to 16+ to match 15+.
